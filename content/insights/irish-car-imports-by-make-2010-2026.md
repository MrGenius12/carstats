This bar chart race tracks car imports into Ireland by make, month by month, from 2010 through July 2026, built from the same dataset behind the CarStats.ie dashboards.

**Volkswagen leads the full period with 159,312 imported vehicles -- nearly one in seven of all imports since 2010.** Ford follows with 113,798, and Audi with 107,629.

**The lead is not the result of one strong year.** Volkswagen has been the most imported make for most of the past decade and a half, consistently ahead of Ford and Audi rather than overtaking them in a single spike.

Toyota (95,485) and BMW (93,772) complete the top five for the period.

**What to look for**

- Volkswagen's consistent gap over Ford and Audi across most years
- How closely Ford and Audi track each other for second and third place
- Where Toyota and BMW sit relative to the top three
