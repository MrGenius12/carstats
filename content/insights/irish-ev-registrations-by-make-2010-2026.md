This bar chart race tracks electric car registrations in Ireland by make, month by month, from 2010 through August 2026, built from the same dataset behind the CarStats.ie dashboards.

**Volkswagen leads with 21,596 cumulative EV registrations, 16.5% of the market.** Tesla and Hyundai are almost tied for second place, both sitting around 14,600 to 14,700.

**The leaderboard isn't the interesting part of this story.** The real movement is underneath the top line: Kia grew 45% year on year, and BYD grew 78% year on year, the fastest of any major make. Nissan was one of the earliest EV makes on Irish roads and has since been overtaken by brands that barely had a presence five years ago.

Total cumulative EV registrations in Ireland now sit at almost 131,000.

**What to look for**

- Volkswagen's lead over a tightly packed Tesla and Hyundai
- BYD's climb from a standing start to the fastest-growing major make
- Nissan's early lead giving way to newer entrants
