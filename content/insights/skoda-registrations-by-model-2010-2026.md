This bar chart race tracks Skoda new car registrations in Ireland by model, month by month, from 2010 through September 2026, built from the same dataset behind the CarStats.ie dashboards. Electric models are highlighted in green, with the electric share of each calendar year shown alongside.

**Skoda has registered 141,782 new cars in Ireland since 2010.** The Octavia accounts for **51,207** of them, about **36%**, and it has led the race in every one of the 201 months.

**The electric story is the interesting part.** Electric models made up **12.4%** of Skoda registrations in 2023, then just **4.4%** in 2024, before recovering to **12.2%** in 2025 and **19.2%** so far in 2026. Skoda's total did not fall in 2024. It rose from 10,180 to 12,182 cars, helped by a strong year for the Octavia (4,458, up from 2,520). The Enyaq dropped from 1,259 registrations in 2023 to 536 in 2024, and the wider Irish electric market also fell that year according to SIMI.

**The Elroq is now adding to the Enyaq.** It registered 741 cars in 2025 and 1,173 so far in 2026, and with the Enyaq on 1,229 the two electric models make up nearly one in five Skoda registrations this year.

**Elsewhere in the table,** the Kodiaq passed the Fabia in January 2025 and the Superb in July 2026.

**What to look for**

- The Octavia holding first place throughout the whole period
- The green electric bars appearing from 2021 and the share dipping in 2024
- The Enyaq and Elroq lifting the electric share to a new high in 2026
- The Kodiaq overtaking the Fabia and then the Superb
