This bar chart race tracks Ireland's best-selling car model each year, from 2010 through July 2026, built from the same registration data behind the CarStats.ie dashboards.

One model dominates most of the period.

**The Volkswagen Golf held the top spot for nine straight years, from 2013 to 2022.** No other model matched that run at number one.

**In 2023, the Hyundai Tucson overtook it and has led ever since.** The Tucson now leads by close to 6,800 units (71,214 versus 64,438).
