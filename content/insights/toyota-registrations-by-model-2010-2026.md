This bar chart race tracks Toyota new car registrations in Ireland by model, from January 2010 through September 2026, built from SIMI registration data processed for the CarStats.ie dashboards.

**Toyota has registered 224,069 new cars in Ireland over the period.** The leaderboard changes hands several times along the way.

**The Avensis leads for six years, then stops at 17,754 when production ends.** The Corolla and Yaris swap first place several times. Since 2019 the Auris hatchback has also been sold as the Corolla, and the Corolla pulls away to 51,263.

**The RAV4 roughly doubles its yearly sales after the boxier 2019 redesign** and climbs from 8th to 3rd. The C-HR, Yaris Cross and Corolla Cross then bring small crossovers to the top, and in early 2026 the Yaris Cross was Ireland's number one car.

**The bZ4X, Toyota's main electric model, has 2,830 registrations, around 1% of the total.**

**What to look for**

- The Avensis leading early on, then stopping when production ends
- The Corolla and Yaris trading first place before the Corolla pulls clear
- The RAV4's climb from 8th to 3rd after the 2019 redesign
- Small crossovers taking over the top of the chart
- The bZ4X's small share of total registrations
