This bar chart race tracks Volkswagen new car registrations in Ireland by model, month by month, from 2010 through September 2026, built from the same dataset behind the CarStats.ie dashboards.

**Volkswagen has registered 216,632 new cars in Ireland since 2010.** One model did a quarter of the job.

**The Golf leads with 56,873 registrations, just over a quarter of all Volkswagen sales in the period.** The Tiguan sits second with 31,626, narrowly ahead of the Polo on 30,702 and the Passat on 27,583. Together the top four models account for close to 70% of everything Volkswagen has registered in Ireland since 2010.

**Among Volkswagen's electric models, the ID.4 leads with 13,521 registrations**, ahead of the ID.3 on 5,208 and the ID.7 and ID.5 further back.

**What to look for**

- The Golf's lasting lead over a crowded chasing pack
- The Tiguan and Polo trading places for second and third
- The ID.4 pulling clear as Volkswagen's leading electric model
