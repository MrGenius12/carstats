CarStats.ie is a free public website with statistics on new and imported car registrations in Ireland. It does not require an account, does not sell personal data, and does not run advertising. This page explains what limited data is collected when the site is used, why, and what choices are available.

## Who is responsible

CarStats.ie is operated by YeS Analytics (Yevhen Sierikov), Co. Wexford, Ireland. For any question about privacy or this policy, or to exercise any of the rights below, contact **[info@carstats.ie](mailto:info@carstats.ie)**.

## What is collected and why

| What | When | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website usage statistics (pages viewed, approximate location by country or city, device and browser type, how the visitor arrived) | When browsing the site | Understanding which pages are useful so the site can be improved | Consent (Google Analytics cookies are only set after "Accept") |
| Feedback message and, if provided, an email address | Only when the feedback form is submitted | Reading and, where an email is given, replying to the feedback | Consent, by choosing to send the form |
| Technical request data such as IP address and browser details | Every request to the site, as with any website | Delivering the site securely and protecting it from abuse | Legitimate interest in running a secure website |

CarStats.ie does not ask for names, does not create user profiles, and does not combine this data with other sources.

## Cookies and similar technologies

When the site is first opened, a banner asks whether analytics cookies may be used.

| Name | Type | Set by | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cookie_consent` | Browser storage (not sent to any server) | CarStats.ie | Remembers the choice made in the cookie banner | Until browser storage is cleared |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*` | Cookie | Google Analytics | Distinguishes visitors and sessions for usage statistics | Up to 2 years, only after "Accept" |

If analytics cookies are declined, Google Analytics runs in a restricted mode: no analytics cookies are set and only limited signals without visitor identifiers are sent. The choice can be changed at any time by clearing this site's data in the browser, which brings the cookie banner back on the next visit.

Cloudflare, which delivers the site, may set strictly necessary cookies used only for security and protection against automated abuse. These do not track visitors across sites.

## Third-party services

The site relies on the following providers. Each processes data under its own privacy terms, and some of them may process data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards required by GDPR, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

- **Google Analytics** (Google Ireland Limited): website usage statistics, as described above.
- **Looker Studio and Google Sheets** (Google): the interactive dashboards and the data update date are loaded from Google. When a dashboard is displayed, Google receives standard request data such as the visitor's IP address.
- **YouTube** (Google): videos in Insights posts are not loaded until the play button is pressed. After that, the video is played through YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com).
- **Formspree**: receives messages sent through the feedback form and forwards them by email.
- **Cloudflare**: delivers and protects the site, and handles email sent to info@carstats.ie.
- **GitHub Pages** (GitHub): hosts the site files.

Links to other websites, such as car marketplaces or vehicle history services, lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

## How long data is kept

- Google Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics and is then deleted automatically.
- Feedback messages are kept in email only as long as needed to read and respond to them, and are deleted from Formspree's archive within 30 days.
- Technical request logs are kept by the hosting and delivery providers for short periods under their own policies.

## Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), visitors have the right to:

- ask for a copy of any personal data held about them;
- ask for inaccurate data to be corrected;
- ask for their data to be deleted;
- object to or restrict certain processing;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal.

To make a request, email **[info@carstats.ie](mailto:info@carstats.ie)**. Visitors also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Commission in Ireland ([dataprotection.ie](https://www.dataprotection.ie/)).
