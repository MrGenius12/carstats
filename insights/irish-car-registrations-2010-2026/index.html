Irish Car Registrations, 2010 to 2026, in Motion

25 July 2026
TrendsMakes & ModelsEngine Type

This visualisation tracks new car registrations in Ireland month by month, from January 2010 through June 2026, compressed into a 100 second overview built directly from the same dataset behind the CarStats.ie dashboards.

Two patterns stand out across the sixteen-year period.

Toyota and Volkswagen have traded the top spot for most of the period. Neither make has held a lasting lead; the two brands swap first place repeatedly across the years, making the market's number one position one of the more contested storylines in the data.

Hyundai's rise is the story of the last decade. Starting from a modest position in 2010, Hyundai climbed steadily year over year and overtook Ford in 2024 to take third place, a position it has held since.

What to look for

  • The back-and-forth between Toyota and Volkswagen for the top spot
  • Hyundai's gradual, uninterrupted climb through the rankings
  • The point in 2024 where Hyundai passes Ford for third place
